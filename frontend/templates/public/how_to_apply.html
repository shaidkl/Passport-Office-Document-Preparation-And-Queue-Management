How to apply for a passport

Follow these steps in order. Your dashboard will always show the next action required.

1

Create and verify your account

Register with correct personal details, then verify your account using the secure link sent to your email.

2

Complete the application

Choose the passport category and service type, then submit accurate application information.

3

Upload documents and photo

Upload clear supporting documents and a valid passport photo. Each file is reviewed individually.

4

Complete the fee payment

After the required files are uploaded, complete the secure eSewa payment so staff review can begin.

5

Wait for document review

Staff approve or reject each document. If one is rejected, upload a new replacement while the old record remains in history.

6

Complete biometrics verification

Authorized staff verify the applicant's biometrics after every current document is approved.

7

Authorize the digital signature

An authorized officer applies the government digital signature after biometrics are verified.

8

Complete queue processing

The signed application receives a processing token and is handled through the staff queue.

9

Generate the passport

After queue processing is completed, the passport is approved and becomes available to generate or download.
